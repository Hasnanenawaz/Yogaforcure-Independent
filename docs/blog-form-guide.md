# Blog form — example content for clients

Use this as a reference when creating a blog post in the admin dashboard (`/admin` → **New blog**).

---

## Title *
**Example:** `Yoga Is Very Beneficial for Health`

Short, clear headline. This becomes the page title and URL slug (e.g. `/blog/yoga-is-very-beneficial-for-health`).

---

## Excerpt *
**Example:**
> A short summary of why regular yoga practice supports body and mind — perfect for the blog listing cards and Google search snippets.

Keep it **1–2 sentences** (about 120–160 characters works well for SEO).

---

## Content *
Write the full article in plain text. **Use a blank line between paragraphs.**

**Example:**

```
Yoga is one of the most holistic practices for physical and mental wellness. Regular practice improves flexibility, strength, and balance while calming the nervous system.

Morning yoga helps you start the day with clarity. Even 15–20 minutes of gentle stretching and breathing can reduce stiffness and improve focus.

For beginners, start with simple poses like Tadasana (Mountain Pose), Balasana (Child's Pose), and gentle seated twists. Always listen to your body and breathe steadily.

Online group sessions are a great way to stay consistent. Practicing with a teacher ensures safe alignment and keeps you motivated week after week.
```

---

## Images
- Upload **2–6 photos** per post (yoga practice, class moments, nature, etc.).
- **Reorder** images with ↑ ↓ — the first image is used on blog cards and social previews.
- Add **alt text** for each image (helps SEO and accessibility).

**Example alt texts:**
- `Neha demonstrating Tadasana in an online yoga class`
- `Students in a live group yoga session on Zoom`
- `Morning sun salutation practice at home`

---

## SEO fields (optional but recommended)

### Meta title
**Example:** `Health Benefits of Yoga | Yoga by Neha`

If left empty, the blog **title** is used.

### Meta description
**Example:**
> Discover how daily yoga improves flexibility, reduces stress, and supports long-term health. Tips from online Indian yoga teacher Neha.

If left empty, the **excerpt** is used.

---

## Publish
- **Unchecked** = Draft (only visible in admin dashboard)
- **Checked** = Published (visible on `/blog` and search engines)

You can also toggle publish status later from the dashboard list.

---

## Quick checklist before publishing
- [ ] Title is clear and specific
- [ ] Excerpt reads well as a preview
- [ ] Content has paragraph breaks
- [ ] At least one image uploaded with alt text
- [ ] Meta title & description filled (optional)
- [ ] **Publish** is checked when ready to go live
